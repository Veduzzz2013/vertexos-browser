Google Cloud & OAuth Data Policy

Vertex OS features an optional cloud filesystem utility that allows users to access their Google Drive files natively within the Vertex file manager.

Limited Use Disclosure: Vertex OS’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How Vertex OS Uses Google Data

  • Requested Scopes: Vertex OS requests access to Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive or drive.file) solely to mount your cloud directory locally into ~/Vertex Cloud.
  • Local Storage Only: All OAuth tokens, credentials, and cached metadata remain strictly on your local machine in encrypted configuration storage.
  • No Third-Party Transmission: Vertex OS has no central servers and does not transmit, log, sell, or analyze your files, email address, or authentication keys.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At Vertex OS, data sovereignty and privacy are foundational. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict commitment to zero data collection.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Vertex OS does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal information, telemetry data, device identifiers, or browsing activity. The operating system contains no built-in tracking mechanisms.

2. Cloud Credentials & Tokens

When you connect third-party accounts (such as Google Drive) via Vertex OS utilities, authentication tokens are saved locally in your user profile. We never receive, relay, or store these credentials on any remote server.

3. Revocation & Control

You retain total ownership over your data. You can disconnect your cloud accounts at any time from the Vertex Control Center or by managing your Google Account security permissions directly.
